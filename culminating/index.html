Item #4

Decision-making and Goals

To create short-term goals, I used the SMART goal-setting strategy: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. This ensures that my goals are clear, have measurable progress, are achievable, are aligned with my future plans, and actually have deadlines.

Academic Goal

90% mark average for the next school year by reviewing concepts for 15 minutes after school each day.

SMART Goal-Setting Strategy

  • S: 90% mark average with 15 minutes of daily review
  • M: Test results and report cards give exact progress indicators
  • A: I have significant influence over my grades
  • R: Good grades indicate understanding of material and effective work habits
  • T: Over the span of the upcoming school year

Athletic Goal

Run 3 km in under 14 minutes by this fall's Cross Country City Championships by running at least a kilometer a week.

SMART Goal-Setting Strategy

  • S: 3 km in under 15 minutes with weekly practice
  • M: I can use a stopwatch to time myself
  • A: The goal only involves a small distance once a week
  • R: I participated on my school's Cross Country team last year and plan to continue doing so
  • T: By the championships this fall

Coding Goal

I will code in Python for 15 minutes each day to make a simple text-based number guessing game by Halloween.

SMART Goal-Setting Strategy

  • S: Text-based number guessing game with 15 minutes of daily learning
  • M: I can make prototypes to see how close I am
  • A: Only 15 minutes daily
  • R: I enjoy programming, and Python is a programming language that I would like to learn
  • T: By Halloween

Item #5

Impact of a Trend on my Future

Trend: "Fewer Jobs for Life"

Summary

The shift away from "jobs for life" results in increased gig/contract work and the need for continuous skill-building. More temporary jobs results in less pressure to commit. Although there's more room for exploration and co-op, people still need to eventually make a choice.

Influence

Co-operative Education

This trend encourages me to take advantage of opportunities like co-operative education and experiential learning. It incentivizes part-time jobs and getting internships early, as opposed to waiting for the "perfect match."

Transferable Skills

This trend also encourages me to become more adaptable and build flexible skills. Instead of just focusing on one niche, I must grow in ways that will apply to multiple possible careers or short-term opportunities.
