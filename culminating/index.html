Problem-solving

I have improved my problem-solving skills over the summer by finding answers to questions I had and paying attention to the questions that others asked. I can demonstrate problem-solving skills by reasonably answering my own questions and focusing more on possible solutions. Problem-solving is an important skill to have in the future, as there won't always be someone to guide every step.
